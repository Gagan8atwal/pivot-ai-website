# Customer Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Pivot AI! This guide gets your AI receptionist live. Most businesses are
up and running quickly with our founder-led setup.

---

## Before you start — gather these
- Your **business name**, hours, and main phone number.
- The **departments/people** calls should route to (name + mobile), e.g. Dispatch,
  Mechanic, Manager.
- Your **top 10–15 FAQs** (services, pricing ranges, service area, "are you hiring", etc.).
- **Google Calendar** you want appointments booked into.
- Preferred **languages** (English, Punjabi, Hindi).

---

## Step 1 — Create your account
Sign up at **pivotcalls.co**, confirm your email, and log in. Your workspace
(tenant) is created automatically.

## Step 2 — Business profile & hours
In **Settings**, set your business name, timezone, contact number, and **operating
hours** (mark closed days). Add an **after-hours** greeting for nights/weekends.

## Step 3 — Greetings & languages
In **Settings → AI receptionist / IVR**, write your **main greeting**, **after-hours
greeting**, and **holiday greeting** in **English, Punjabi, and Hindi**. Toggle
**AI-answers-first** and choose warm transfer vs. conference.

## Step 4 — Departments & routing
Add your menu options (e.g. **Press 1 Dispatch, 2 Mechanic, 3 Manager**) with each
destination phone number in E.164 format (e.g. +15550001234). Enable warm transfer
where you want a live handoff.

## Step 5 — Knowledge base
In **Knowledge Base**, add FAQs, services, and policies so the AI answers common
questions in your words. Start with your top 10–15; you can add more anytime.

## Step 6 — Calendar & confirmations
Connect the **Google Calendar** for bookings. Confirm your **SMS and email
confirmation** templates so customers get an instant receipt.

## Step 7 — Route your calls
Either **forward** your existing number to Pivot AI (call-forwarding code, provided
during setup) or use a **Pivot AI number**. Test by calling in yourself.

## Step 8 — Test & go live
Place a few test calls: after-hours, a booking, and a department transfer. Watch the
**Dashboard** and **CRM** populate. Adjust greetings/KB as needed. You're live. 🎉

---

## Where to look after go-live
- **Dashboard** — today's leads and upcoming appointments at a glance.
- **CRM** — every lead, pipeline, notes, and follow-up tasks.
- **Appointments / Calls / Messages** — activity and confirmations.
- **Team** — invite staff and set roles (admin/manager can edit; members read-only).
- **Billing** — plan, invoices, upgrades (owner).

## Tips
- Keep greetings short and warm; lead with your business name.
- Revisit the knowledge base monthly — add the questions callers actually ask.
- Use **tasks** in the CRM so no lead goes cold.
