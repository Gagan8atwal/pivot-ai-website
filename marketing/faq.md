# Pivot AI — FAQ

## The basics

**What is Pivot AI?**
A 24/7 AI receptionist for local service businesses. It answers your calls in a
natural voice, captures the lead, books appointments, and sends confirmations.

**What exactly does it do on a call?**
Greets the caller in your business's name, answers common questions from your
knowledge base, captures their name/number/reason, books an appointment when
appropriate, and can warm-transfer to the right person during business hours.

**Is it a robot menu (press 1, press 2)?**
It can offer a simple menu **and** hold a natural conversation. You choose whether
the AI answers first, and how calls route.

## Setup & calls

**How do my calls reach Pivot AI?**
Either forward your existing number to Pivot AI, or use a Pivot AI number. We walk
you through it during onboarding.

**How long does setup take?**
Most businesses are live quickly with our founder-led setup — greetings, hours,
departments, calendar, and a starter knowledge base.

**Can it book appointments?**
Yes — it checks availability and books into your **Google Calendar**, then texts and
emails the customer a confirmation.

**What languages does it speak?**
English, Punjabi, and Hindi.

**Can it transfer to a real person?**
Yes — set up departments (e.g. Dispatch / Mechanic / Manager) with warm transfer
during business hours.

**What happens after hours or on holidays?**
It uses your after-hours / holiday greeting, still captures the lead, and can take a
message or book for the next available slot.

## Data & control

**Where do my leads go?**
Into your Pivot AI dashboard and CRM — with call transcripts — so you see every lead
in one place.

**Can my team use it?**
Yes. Invite team members with roles: admins/managers can edit settings; other members
have read access.

**Do I stay in control of what it says?**
Completely. You set the greetings, hours, routing, and knowledge base. Update anytime.

## Billing

**How much does it cost?**
Plans start at **$49/month** (see the pricing sheet). 14-day free trial, no credit
card to start, cancel anytime. Billing is via Stripe.

**Is there a contract?**
No — month-to-month. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from the billing page.

**What if I go over my plan's call volume?**
We'll help you pick the right plan for your volume; you can upgrade anytime.

## Trust

**Will callers know it's AI?**
We recommend being transparent; you control the greeting wording. Many callers simply
appreciate that someone picked up and helped.

**Is it reliable?**
It answers every call, every hour — no sick days, no hold queue, no missed voicemail.
