# Pivot AI — Demo Script

A ~10-minute live or recorded demo. Goal: prospect *sees and hears* a missed call
become a booked job. Keep it about **their** business.

---

## 0. Before the demo (2 min prep)
- Have a working AI number/config with the prospect's trade in mind.
- Know their **average job value** and **rough call volume** (ask on the call).
- Open the **Dashboard** and **CRM** in the background, ready to show live.

## 1. Frame (30s)
> "Quick question — when you're on a job and the phone rings, what happens to that
> call right now?"

Let them answer (usually: voicemail / missed / spouse takes it). Reflect it back:
> "Right — and a lot of those callers just dial the next company. Let me show you how
> Pivot AI catches those."

## 2. The live call (3 min) — the hero moment
Call the AI on speaker so they hear it.
- Let the AI **greet** in the business name.
- Ask a **common question** (hours, service area) → AI answers from the knowledge base.
- Ask to **book an appointment** → AI checks availability and books.
- (Optional) Trigger a **department transfer** or ask in **Punjabi/Hindi**.

Narrate lightly: "Notice it sounded natural, answered the question, and booked me."

## 3. Show the result (2 min)
Switch to the screen:
- **Dashboard** — the new lead + upcoming appointment appear.
- **CRM** — open the lead: name, number, reason, transcript.
- **Appointments** — the booking, synced to Google Calendar.
- Mention the **SMS/email confirmation** the "customer" just got.

> "That entire lead was captured and booked without you touching the phone."

## 4. Make it theirs (2 min)
- "You'd set the greeting, hours, and the questions it answers — in your words."
- "Calls route to your team: Dispatch, Mechanic, Manager — however you run it."
- "It speaks English, Punjabi, and Hindi."
- Tie to ROI: "If you miss ~X calls a week and a job's worth ~$Y, here's what
  recovering even a few does…" (do the quick math out loud).

## 5. Close (1 min)
> "Want me to set this up for your business? It's a 14-day free trial, no card to
> start. I can configure your greetings and calendar with you today."

Book the trial/onboarding then and there. Send the follow-up sequence (see
follow-up-email-sequence.md).

---

### If something isn't available in the demo
Be honest: "That specific view isn't in today's build — here's how it works," then
show the closest real screen. Never invent a feature. (Only demo what's listed in
README.md → verified capabilities.)
