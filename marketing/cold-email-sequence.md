# Cold Email Sequence — 4 touches

Audience: owners/operators of local service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, restoration,
roofing, auto, etc.). Tone: short, specific, no hype. Personalize `{{First}}`,
`{{Company}}`, `{{Trade}}`, `{{City}}`. One idea per email. Always CAN-SPAM compliant
(real name, address, one-click unsubscribe).

> Only claim verified capabilities (see README.md). Don't cite made-up stats.

### Cadence & tips
Day 1 → 3 → 7 → 12. Send Tue–Thu mornings. Stop the sequence on any reply. Keep each
email under ~120 words. Lead with the prospect's problem, not the product.
