### The honest summary
- **Voicemail** is free but most callers hang up and dial your competitor.
- **Answering services** cover the phone but often bill per minute, use generic
  scripts, and hand you patchy lead data.
- **A receptionist** is great — during business hours, for one call at a time, at
  full salary + benefits + coverage gaps.
- **Pivot AI** answers every call 24/7, captures the lead, books the job, confirms
  by text/email, speaks EN/PA/HI, and routes the important calls to your team — for
  a flat monthly price.

*Pivot AI complements a human team — it catches everything after hours, during
rushes, and on the calls nobody got to.*
