# Pivot AI — Sales Deck

12 slides. Each slide lists **headline**, **on-slide copy**, and **talk track**.
Design per `/brand/BRAND_GUIDE.md` (navy + amber, Inter). Keep ~20–25 words on-slide.

---

### Slide 1 — Title
**Never miss another customer call.**
*Copy:* Pivot AI — the 24/7 AI receptionist for local service businesses. · pivotcalls.co
*Talk:* "I'll show you how you can answer every call — even the ones you miss today — without hiring anyone."

### Slide 2 — The problem
**Every missed call is a missed job.**
*Copy:* You're on a job, it's after hours, or two calls come at once. The caller hits voicemail — and dials your competitor.
*Talk:* Make it concrete for their trade. Ask: "How many calls a week go to voicemail?"

### Slide 3 — The cost
**Do the math.**
*Copy:* If one job is worth $300–$3,000, even a few recovered calls a month pays for itself many times over.
*Talk:* Anchor on *their* average job value. This is the emotional + rational hook.

### Slide 4 — Meet Pivot AI
**A receptionist that never sleeps.**
*Copy:* Answers 24/7 in a natural voice, in your business's name. No voicemail. No hold music.
*Talk:* Position as always-on teammate, not a robot menu.

### Slide 5 — What it does (1)
**Answers & captures.**
*Copy:* Greets callers, handles the conversation, logs name + number + reason to your dashboard — with a transcript.
*Talk:* Emphasize you finally *see* every lead in one place.

### Slide 6 — What it does (2)
**Books & confirms.**
*Copy:* Checks availability, books into Google Calendar, and texts + emails the customer a confirmation.
*Talk:* This is the "it closes the loop" moment.

### Slide 7 — Speaks their language
**English · Punjabi · Hindi.**
*Copy:* Greets and converses multilingually — meet more of your customers where they are.
*Talk:* Strong differentiator for many local markets.

### Slide 8 — Routing & control
**The right call gets to the right person.**
*Copy:* Simple menu + warm transfer (Dispatch / Mechanic / Manager). Set hours, after-hours, greetings, and a knowledge base.
*Talk:* Reassure: you stay in control; the AI follows your rules.

### Slide 9 — The dashboard
**Every call, lead, and booking in one place.**
*Copy:* Dashboard + CRM with pipeline, notes, tasks, and call/message activity.
*Talk:* Show a screenshot (see product-screenshots.md).

### Slide 10 — How it works
**Live in a few simple steps.**
*Copy:* 1) We configure your AI. 2) Forward your calls. 3) Leads & bookings roll in.
*Talk:* Lower the effort perception — founder-led setup.

### Slide 11 — Pricing
**Flat, simple plans.**
*Copy:* Starter $49 · Pro $149 · Premium $299 /mo. 14-day free trial, no card to start, cancel anytime.
*Talk:* Tie plan to their call volume. (Confirm final pricing — see pricing-sheet.md.)

### Slide 12 — Call to action
**Let's set up your AI receptionist.**
*Copy:* Start your 14-day free trial today. · pivotcalls.co · hello@pivotcalls.co
*Talk:* Book the trial/onboarding now; offer to configure it together.
