# Objection-Handling Guide

Framework: **Acknowledge → Reframe → Evidence → Ask.** Stay honest — only promise
what the product does (see README.md). Never bluff a feature.

---

### 1. "My customers hate talking to a robot / AI."
- **Acknowledge:** Fair — nobody likes a clunky phone tree.
- **Reframe:** The alternative right now is *voicemail*, which callers like even less —
  most just hang up and call the next shop.
- **Evidence:** Pivot AI answers in a natural voice, in your name, and answers real
  questions. You write the greeting; you decide how it sounds.
- **Ask:** "Can I call it live so you can hear it for 30 seconds?"

### 2. "It's too expensive."
- **Acknowledge:** Budget matters.
- **Reframe:** Compare it to *one* lost job, not to $0. Plans start at $49/mo.
- **Evidence:** If a job is worth hundreds to thousands, recovering a few missed calls
  a month pays for it many times over — with no salary, benefits, or turnover.
- **Ask:** "What's your average job worth? Let's do the quick math."

### 3. "I already have voicemail / an answering service."
- **Acknowledge:** Good that calls are covered somehow.
- **Reframe:** Voicemail loses most callers; answering services often bill per minute
  and hand you thin lead data.
- **Evidence:** Pivot AI answers 24/7, captures structured leads to a dashboard, books
  to your calendar, and confirms by text/email — flat monthly price.
- **Ask:** "Want to see the lead + booking it creates automatically?"

### 4. "I don't have time to set it up."
- **Acknowledge:** You're busy running the business.
- **Reframe:** That's exactly the point — and setup is founder-led.
- **Evidence:** We configure greetings, hours, departments, calendar, and a starter
  knowledge base *with* you in a short call.
- **Ask:** "Give me 20 minutes and I'll get you live — when works?"

### 5. "Will it book on my calendar correctly / double-book?"
- **Acknowledge:** Scheduling has to be reliable.
- **Reframe:** It books against your real availability.
- **Evidence:** Appointments sync to your **Google Calendar**, and the customer gets an
  SMS/email confirmation so everyone's aligned.
- **Ask:** "Want me to book a test appointment live right now?"

### 6. "What about after hours / holidays?"
- **Evidence:** You set after-hours and holiday greetings; it still captures the lead
  and can book the next slot. That's often when the *most* calls go unanswered today.
- **Ask:** "How many calls come in after 5pm or on weekends?"

### 7. "My callers speak Punjabi / Hindi."
- **Evidence:** Pivot AI greets and converses in **English, Punjabi, and Hindi** — a
  real edge in many local markets.
- **Ask:** "Want to hear it greet a caller in Punjabi?"

### 8. "I need calls to reach my techs, not just an AI."
- **Evidence:** Set up department routing (Dispatch / Mechanic / Manager) with **warm
  transfer** during business hours. AI handles the rest.
- **Ask:** "Who should the urgent calls go to? Let's map it."

### 9. "How do I know it actually works?"
- **Evidence:** Live demo call + a 14-day free trial (no card to start). You'll see real
  leads in your dashboard within days.
- **Ask:** "Want to start the trial and judge it on real calls?"

### 10. "Is my data / my customers' data safe?"
- **Acknowledge:** Right to ask.
- **Evidence:** Leads live in your private workspace; billing is handled by **Stripe**
  (we don't store card numbers). Be transparent about AI in your greeting if you prefer.
- **Ask:** "Any specific requirement I should confirm for you?"

---

### Golden rules
- If you don't know, say so and follow up — don't invent.
- Reframe against the *real* alternative (voicemail), not perfection.
- Whenever possible, **let the product talk**: play a live call, show a real lead.
