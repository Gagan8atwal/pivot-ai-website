# Follow-up / Nurture Sequence

Post-demo and trial nurture. Goal: move from "interested" → "live and paying." Keep it
helpful, not pushy. Personalize `{{First}}`, `{{Company}}`.

---

## A) After a demo (no trial started yet)

### Day 0 — Recap (send within an hour)
**Subject:** your Pivot AI recap + next step
Hi {{First}}, great talking through {{Company}}'s calls. Quick recap of what Pivot AI
would do for you:
- Answer every call 24/7 in your name
- Capture the lead + book to your Google Calendar
- Text/email the customer a confirmation
- Speak English/Punjabi/Hindi and route to your team

Next step: I'll set up your 14-day free trial (no card). Want me to start it today?
— {{SenderName}} · pivotcalls.co

### Day 2 — Remove friction
**Subject:** I'll do the setup for you
Hi {{First}}, the setup is the easy part — I'll configure your greetings, hours,
departments, and a starter knowledge base with you in one short call. 20 minutes and
you're live. Grab a time: {{scheduling link}}.

### Day 5 — Proof + urgency (soft)
**Subject:** every week is missed calls
Hi {{First}}, every week without this is calls going to voicemail. The trial's free
for 14 days and cancel-anytime — the only risk is finding out how many calls you're
missing. Want me to start it?

## B) During the free trial

### Trial Day 1 — Welcome
**Subject:** you're live 🎉 here's where to look
You're set up! Watch your **Dashboard** for today's leads and **CRM** for the details
+ transcripts. Reply here anytime and I'll tune greetings or your knowledge base.

### Trial Day 4 — Show value
**Subject:** here's what Pivot AI caught for {{Company}}
Hi {{First}}, quick nudge to check your dashboard — leads captured and any bookings so
far. Anything sound off in the greetings? I'll adjust it. Want to add more FAQs so it
answers even more on its own?

### Trial Day 10 — Convert
**Subject:** keep it running after {{trial end date}}?
Hi {{First}}, your trial ends {{date}}. To keep every call answered, pick a plan on
the **Billing** page (Starter/Pro/Premium) — month-to-month, cancel anytime. Want a
hand choosing based on your call volume?

### Trial Day 13 — Last call
**Subject:** don't lose your AI receptionist tomorrow
Hi {{First}}, your trial ends tomorrow. One click on the Billing page keeps Pivot AI
answering for {{Company}}. Happy to hop on if you have any last questions.

---

## C) Won-back / dormant (if trial lapsed)
**Subject:** want your calls covered again?
Hi {{First}}, no pressure — if you're still missing calls, your Pivot AI setup is
ready to switch back on. Reply "reactivate" and I'll handle it.

---

### Tips
Stop the sequence the moment they convert or reply. Reference **real** activity from
their dashboard where possible. Keep it short and human.
