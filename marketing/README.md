## Verified product capabilities (the only claims we make)
- **24/7 AI call answering** — never miss a call, day or night.
- **Lead capture** — caller name, number, and reason logged to a dashboard/CRM.
- **Appointment booking** with **Google Calendar** sync.
- **SMS + email confirmations** to the customer.
- **Multilingual**: English, Punjabi, Hindi.
- **Department routing / warm transfer** (e.g. Dispatch, Mechanic, Manager).
- **Configurable greetings, IVR, business hours & after-hours** handling.
- **Knowledge base** so the AI answers common questions in your words.
- **Dashboard / CRM** with pipeline, notes, tasks, call & message activity.
- **Stripe** billing.
